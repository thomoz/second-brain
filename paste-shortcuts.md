## Types of Trades:

Buy Stop order (Heartbeat breakout):

Example: a share is currently $50, but you only want to buy if it rises to $55 (when it breaks out above heartbeat pattern)

Set:

Action: Buy
Order type: STP (Stop)
Stop price: $55

The order sits inactive while the price is below $55. Once the market reaches the stop price, it triggers and becomes a market buy order.

If you want more control over the purchase price, use STP LMT (Stop Limit) instead. For example:

Stop = $55.00
Limit = $55.30

That means: once it hits $55, buy — but don't pay more than $55.30.
