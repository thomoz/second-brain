HDV (defensive: staples):

A low-cost, defensive ETF that tracks 75 high-yielding, financially healthy U.S. companies to provide steady quarterly income with lower market volatility.

The fund actively targets mature, cash-rich, defensive giants that dominate their respective markets. To make the list, companies must pass Morningstar's strict "economic moat" test (meaning they have a strong competitive advantage) and maintain a low risk of bankruptcy.

Energy Titans: Major oil and gas corporations with massive cash flows, such as ExxonMobil and Chevron.

Consumer Staples: Legacy household brands that people buy regardless of economic conditions, like Procter & Gamble, Coca-Cola, and Philip Morris.

Healthcare Giants: Established pharmaceutical and medical device manufacturers with stable profit margins, such as AbbVie and Johnson & Johnson.

Utilities & Financials: Heavily regulated, steady businesses like electrical grid providers and large insurance or banking firms
