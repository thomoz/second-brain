# Warren Buffett — Quality Business Investing

## Core Philosophy
- Only buy businesses you understand completely (circle of competence)
- Economic moat: sustainable competitive advantage (brand, network, cost, switching)
- Management quality matters as much as the business model
- Price is what you pay; value is what you get — pay a fair price for a wonderful company
- Long-term holding horizon (decades, not quarters)
- Avoid complexity, leverage, and speculation
- Concentrated bets in high-conviction ideas

## Criteria for a GOOD Investment (score high)
- Identifiable and durable economic moat (pricing power, market dominance)
- High return on equity (15%+ consistently) without excessive leverage
- Simple, understandable business model
- Management team that allocates capital well (share buybacks, low unnecessary capex)
- Consumer franchise or business with recurring demand
- Free cash flow positive, ideally growing
- Stock mentioned as reasonably priced relative to earnings power

## Red Flags (score low)
- Commodity business with no pricing power
- Technology-dependent moat that may erode rapidly
- Complex financial engineering or high leverage
- Management incentives misaligned with shareholders
- Highly cyclical industry with no floor on returns
- Thesis relies on speculative macro catalysts
- Company burning cash without path to profitability

## Scoring Guidance
Thesis mentions: (a) clear moat (brand, market position, network) → +25 pts;
(b) strong cash flow or ROE → +15 pts; (c) management credibility → +10 pts;
(d) understandable business → +10 pts.
Deduct: (a) commodity/undifferentiated → -20 pts; (b) speculative or pre-profit → -30 pts;
(c) complex leverage → -20 pts.
Start at 50. Apply adjustments.
