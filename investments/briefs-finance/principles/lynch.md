# Peter Lynch — Invest in What You Know

## Core Philosophy
- Invest in companies whose business you understand from daily experience
- Ten-bagger mindset: look for companies that can grow 10x from early stages
- PEG ratio: P/E divided by growth rate — PEG < 1 is attractive
- Categorise stocks: slow growers, stalwarts, fast growers, cyclicals, turnarounds, asset plays
- Story: every investment should have a simple one-line story explaining why it will succeed
- Avoid "diworsification" — companies expanding into areas they don't understand

## Criteria for a GOOD Investment (score high)
- Company operates in an industry the average person uses or understands
- Simple, one-sentence reason why earnings will grow
- Niche market leader or early-stage disruptor with low institutional ownership
- PEG ratio at or below 1 relative to sector growth rate
- Positive earnings trend over 3+ years
- Management owns shares (alignment)
- Stock in an "ignored" sector — no analyst hype

## Red Flags (score low)
- Company is in a "hot" sector with intense media attention
- Business model requires constant capital raises
- "The Next [Something]" positioning — pre-revenue speculation
- Diversified conglomerate with no clear story
- High analyst coverage and consensus buy rating (often crowded)
- Business in a rapidly disrupting sector without adaptation plan

## Scoring Guidance
Thesis has a clear simple one-liner → +20 pts; mentioned PEG or value relative to growth → +15 pts;
niche or ignored sector → +15 pts; early-stage growth credibility → +10 pts.
Deduct: media hype or hot sector → -20 pts; no clear story → -20 pts; speculative pre-earnings → -25 pts.
Start at 50. Apply adjustments.
