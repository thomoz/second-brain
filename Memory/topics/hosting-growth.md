# Project: Hosting Business Growth

## Goal
Increase shows + find higher-paying venues to build financial buffer before potential market downturn.
Target: more cash to bank + capital ready to deploy into shares post-crash.

## Strategy
- Replace low-margin/distant shows with closer, higher-paying ones
- Improve venue sales conversion (currently relying on website funnel)
- Reduce time cost of show promotion (ad creation is a bottleneck)

## Current Bottlenecks
- Time-poor: some shows 2-2.5hrs away
- Ad creation slow: extracting video frames → templates → posting to FB groups
- Tried 3rd-party AI ad tools — quality worse than manual process
- Venue sales is weak despite strong reputation (4.9★, 76+ Google reviews)
