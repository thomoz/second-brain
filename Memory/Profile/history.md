# History

_Built through "Ask Me Questions" sessions. Append with dates — history matters._

## Background

## 2026-06-18 Update

- Self-described introvert who was always looking for a way off the stage
- Born/raised in NZ; moved to Australia in 2001 with partner Kristie (to be closer to her family)
- Lives in Kiama, NSW (at least as of karaoke start in 2008; 2 min from Kiama Grand Hotel)

## Key Turning Points

## 2026-06-18 Update

### 1990s — Performing trio (NZ)
- Part of a trio performing tribute shows around NZ pubs, then picked up by a management company
- Same circuit as the management company's own ABBA tribute show
- Residency replacement at Twiggers (theatre restaurant, Addington Raceway, Christchurch)
- Shows produced: rock & roll tribute (2 NZ tours), country show (1 tour), Priscilla Queen of the Desert (2 shows), male strip review "The Naughty Boys of New Zealand" (2 NZ tours)

### Duo "Celebrity" + Move to Australia (2001)
- Started duo "Celebrity" with one of the trio members (Kristie); entered relationship with her
- Moved to Australia in 2001 to be closer to her family

### Solo show "Gilbert" (2001–2008)
- Created solo character show — Gilbert, a geeky character who acts like a rock star
- Made a full-time living from the Gilbert Show until the GFC (2008)

### GFC Pivot → Karaoke (2008)
- GFC hit: nearly 3 months with no work
- Started karaoke shows as a pivot; within 2 weeks had a residency at Kiama Grand Hotel
- Karaoke became very successful; expanded to multiple venues
- Started hosting trivia at Kiama Grand Hotel in 2010

### The Social Network moment → SongbookDB (late 2010)
- Saw "The Social Network" late 2010 — sparked thinking about what app he could build
- Concept: replace karaoke songbooks (logical given his industry knowledge)
- ~1 year of trial and error before commercial release
- Built out software suite with two joint ventures:
  - Digital One Audio (PCDJ line) → "Karaoki" karaoke hosting software
  - MTU → "Hoster" karaoke hosting software

## How I Got Here

- Reluctant performer who used the stage to earn a living while always seeking something else
- Software business (SongbookDB) born from GFC-era disruption and a movie that sparked an idea
- Live entertainment businesses expanded out of the same original karaoke pivot in 2008
