# Personality

_Built through "Ask Me Questions" sessions. Append with dates — history matters._

## How I Work Best

## 2026-06-18 Update

- Works best alone
- Best flow state: creative work — songwriting, recording, and animated movie project (Juno: Wonderdog)
- Introverted by nature; the performing and hosting work has always been against his natural grain
- Self-described introvert who was "always looking for something to replace" stage work

## What Energises Me

## 2026-06-18 Update

- Solo creative work: songwriting, music recording, animation/storytelling projects
- Building things (software, creative projects) that exist independently of his physical presence

## What Drains Me

## 2026-06-18 Update

- Hosting shows is the primary drain — despite being good at it and well-liked by patrons
- Stepping into the host role requires deliberate energy; leaving is a relief
- Dreads post-show social interaction — people approaching him after the night is over
- After shows: needs to decompress alone; finds it hard to be present even with girlfriend and stepson the night of or the day after a show
- Classic introvert recovery pattern: social performance → energy debt → solo recharge required

## Decision Style

## 2026-06-18 Update

- Comfortable connecting with people when he has something concrete to connect over (a project, a product, a reason)
- Finds cold connection/networking uncomfortable — context and purpose make social engagement feel natural
- Ideal lifestyle: creative work that can be commercialized, with selective outbound promotion only when there's a genuine reason to reach out
- The 2-days/week creative time goal is as much emotional/psychological as it is financial
