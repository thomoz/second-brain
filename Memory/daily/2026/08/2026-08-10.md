**[WhatsApp]** 61410868612@c.us: What is my car hire entitlements on an NRMA comprehensive car insurance policy?
**[Brain]** If you have standard NRMA Comprehensive, your hire car entitlement is usually:

If you are not at fault:
hire car / transport is included
up to $75 a day
for up to 21 days
you generally need the other driver’s name and address and NRMA must accept they were at fault

If your car is stolen:
hire car / transport is included
up to $75 a day
for up to 21 days, or until recovered/repaired or claim settled

If the accident is your fault or it is another covered event:
only if you added the hire car option to the policy

If you have Comprehensive Plus instead:
hire car is automatically included after any covered incident
up to $100 a day
no day limit stated, until repaired or claim settled

If you have Comprehensive Basics:
no hire car entitlement

Best check:
your exact PDS or policy schedule in your NRMA account, because options matter
