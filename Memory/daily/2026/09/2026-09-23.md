**[Brain]** Most likely it’s not the petrol engine, since it happens on both petrol and electric.

Common suspects:

- Wheel bearing: pitch rises with road speed, often sounds like a low drone/whirr.
- CVT/transaxle or differential bearing: more likely if the sound changes noticeably when accelerating versus backing off.
- Driveshaft/CV joint: can whine or hum under load.
- Tyres: uneven wear can sound like a bearing, but usually doesn’t change as much with accelerator load.
- Hybrid inverter/motor whine: possible, but usually higher pitched.

Key clue: if the pitch follows car speed, it’s probably wheels/drivetrain. If it follows engine revs, it’s engine/accessory related.

I’d get it checked soon, especially because load-change noise can point to transaxle/drivetrain bearings. If it gets louder quickly, you feel vibration, hear grinding, or see hybrid/warning lights, avoid driving it until inspected.
