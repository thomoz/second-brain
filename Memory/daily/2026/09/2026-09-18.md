## 15:49
**[WhatsApp]** 61410868612@c.us: I have an Apple iPhone 16 but I'm connecting to an android screen in my car
**[Brain]** That should be fine. Your iPhone 16 can connect to many Android-based car head units using Apple CarPlay, usually by USB or wireless CarPlay.

Key point: the car screen may run Android, but your phone still needs CarPlay support on the head unit.

Try:
1. Check the car screen has “CarPlay” app/icon.
2. Use a good USB-C cable first.
3. On iPhone: Settings > General > CarPlay.
4. If wireless, enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, then pair from the head unit.

If it only supports Android Auto and not CarPlay, the iPhone won’t mirror properly without a CarPlay adapter/dongle.

**[Brain]** Short answer: not through normal Apple CarPlay.

CarPlay from an iPhone does not allow YouTube/video apps on the CarPlay interface. Apple limits CarPlay to driving-safe apps like maps, music, calls, messages, podcasts, etc.

But if the “Android screen” is an aftermarket head unit/tablet, you may be able to watch YouTube another way:

Use the Android screen’s own YouTube app if it has internet.

Use screen mirroring/AirPlay from the iPhone if the unit supports it.

Use a CarPlay AI box/dongle that runs Android apps separately.

Important catch: YouTube on the car screen while driving may be blocked, unsafe, or illegal depending on setup. It’s usually only intended for parked use.
